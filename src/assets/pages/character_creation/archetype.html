Archetypes consist of two parts: An overarching role, and a specialization for that role. When choosing an archetype, before spending any Karma, you first choose a role, add the listed skills as archetype skills, and then add one rank in three of these skills to your character. Afterwards, you choose a specialization, add their listed skills to your character's list of archetype skills, and then add one rank to two of these skills. Archetype skills can be noted with a small • next to the skill.

Lastly, you add the archetype bonus listed in the specialization to your character. Typically, your archetype bonus is roughly the equivalent to 10 points of Burden, so keep that in mind if you want to change your archetype bonus (with the Gamemasters discretion).

Playing Awakened and Emergent characters

If you decide to play an Awakened character, choose one of the archetypes in the list above, and switch one of the listed archetype skills with a magical skill, as listed in the table below.

Wanted Skill Switch out...
Astral Any general skill
Spellcasting Any combat skill
Summoning Any combat skill
Qi-Magic Any combat or social skill

Characters that have switched a mundane archetype skill for a magical archetype skill do not gain their typical archetype bonus. Instead, they gain the Awakened trait, which enables them to use talents and equipment only usable for the Awakened, such as foci.

Most Awakened are Aspected, meaning that they only have access to a single magical skill. If they wish to have access to more than just one skill, they must take a rank in the talent "Expanded Aspect". At character creation, this talent can be taken without the usual limits for taking talents during character creation, and without building the required foundation of the talent pyramid first.

Similar to the Awakened, players wanting to play a technomancer (also known as the Emergent) choose one of the listed archetypes, and switch any one combat or social archetype skill for the Resonance skill. Instead of switching their archetype bonus for the Awakened trait, they instead gain the Emergent trait.

Rules for Creating Custom Archetypes

When creating an archetype, each character gains ten archetype skills. These skills are associated with your characters general career, and taking ranks in these are generally cheaper than taking ranks in non-archetype skills.

When creating a custom Archetype for your character, choose 6 general skills that define the general archetype of the character. Then, choose 4 more skills, and you...

  • ...must have at least 1 social or combat skill,
  • ...must have at least 1 knowledge skill,
  • ...can’t have more than 3 combat skills (Hacking, Spellcasting, Summoning and Resonance all count as a combat skill),
  • ...can’t have more than 3 social skills,
  • ...can’t have more than 4 knowledge skills,
  • ...can’t have more than 2 additional general skills

These skills broadly represent a specialization of your character within its base archetype.
