Open Sesame

Tier: 1
Activation: Passive

You can use the Charm skill when using the Take Over hacking action. Additionally, you can use the Tap into Resonance maneuver to add [[w]] to such Charm checks.

Skinlink

Tier: 1
Activation: Passive

As a maneuver, you can touch a device you are Engaged with to upgrade the ability of your next Hacking skill check once. If the device is held by an adversary, or is able to defend itself against being touched, you make a Brawl combat check against the target as part of the Skinlink maneuver.

Resonance Spike

Tier: 1
Activation: Active (Maneuver)

Commit [[p]] from your Resonance dice pool; until the end of the encounter, you can use your Willpower characteristic instead of Intellect for determining the dice pool and base damage of your Data Spike matrix actions, and if you do, you can add [[w]] to those rolls.

Resonatic Cybercompatibility

Tier: 1
Activation: Active (Maneuver)

You can commit an amount of [[p]] up to your Willpower from your Resonance dice pool; you may choose an amount of bodytech whose essence loss is lower or equal to the amount of [[p]] committed, and ignore all penalties towards your Resonance skill checks due to essence loss caused by the chosen implants.
