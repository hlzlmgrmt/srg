All matrix checks fall into one of two categories: Matrix combat checks, and Matrix action checks. Matrix combat checks are made when you want to apply brute force against your target. They are come out fast, and essentially throw your malicious code at your adversaries, and are usually best used when drek has already hit the wall. Like the name implies, they are treated as combat checks with a range of Long, and their difficulty is usually equal to normal combat checks, with a targets Firewall rating acting as both Soak and Defense against suffered system strain and hits.

Matrix actions encompass all other types of checks. They are usually much more stealthy that Matrix combat checks, but usually require you or your persona to be within Short range of your target. When you make a Matrix action against a target - usually because you want to access a device, host or files from within said device or host without proper authorization - you make an Easy ([[d]]) Hacking or Computers skill check, and increase the difficulty by one for each point of Firewall the target has (you do not add [[s]] per point of Firewall like you would with Matrix combat checks).

The Gamemaster can increase or decrease the difficulty of the check, or add additional [[b]] or [[s]] based on environmental details; the total of these effects are too numerous to list here, but you and your Gamemaster can use the table "Difficulty of Matrix skill checks" to get a rough overview of example difficulties and modifiers.
