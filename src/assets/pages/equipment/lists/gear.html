Additionally to the information listed on normal gear entries, toxins provide two additional pieces of information:

Vector: Each toxin has an entry vector to be effective. Some venoms have to be injected, while some have to be inhaled, consumed or similar.

Difficulty: Poisons and Toxins always require a skill check to be resisted by the victim (usually Resilience). The difficulty of the check is represented by this table shorthand, and usually poisons that require a more difficult check to resist are more effective to the victim.
