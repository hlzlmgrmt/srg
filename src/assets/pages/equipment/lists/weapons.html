Brawl Weapons

Brawling weapons are used to improve on hand-to-hand combat. They are usually used with the Brawl skill against a target within Engaged ([[dd]]) range. Unless otherwise noted (or at the Gamemasters discretion), the hands of someone wielding a Brawl weapon stays free, enabling them to interact with objects.

Melee Weapons

Melee weapons encompass all one- and two-handed weapons used for personal combat, such as knives, swords, electric shock prods and shields. They are used with the Melee skill, and - unless otherwise noted - can only be used while Engaged ([[dd]]) with a target.

One-Handed Ranged Weapons

One-handed ranged weapons, such as tasers, pistols and mini-crossbows are light weapons, either primarily used as sidearms for non-combat heavy scenarios or utilized for more inconspicuous work. Characters using one-handed ranged weapons use the Ranged (Light) skill.

Two-Handed Ranged Weapons

These weapons encompass all weapons that require to be shot with both hands, such as submachine guns, rifles, shotguns, and bows. Two-Handed ranged weapons often employ much more power and utility than their one-handed counterparts, but aren’t very subtle, so forget smuggling on of these into your fancy gala. Unless otherwise noted, two-handed ranged weapons use the Ranged (Heavy) skill.

Heavy Ranged Weapons

Heavy ranged weapons are bulky pieces of equipment, trading combat utility for raw firepower. Usually, these weapons are designed to be bolted to vehicles or used with special harnesses, but exceptionally strong individuals (and trolls) are able to withstand the recoil of their bug guns without much effort. Characters using heavy ranged weapons use the Gunnery skill.

Grenades

Grenades are explosive devices, usually intended to inflict damage in an area around their impact point or to smoke people from highly fortified positions. Grenades are usually thrown using the Ranged (Light) skill, but they can also be used with a Grenade Launcher, significantly increasing their range.

Grenades can be triggered in multiple ways: They can be triggered at impact, which causes them to explode immediately after contact with their opponents, they can be triggered with a time trigger, allowing them to bounce off surfaces before exploding after a set amount of time (usually a few seconds), they can be triggered using a signal through the Matrix, allowing them to be remote triggered after being placed on their intended spot, or they can be automatically triggered through visual or persona-scanning sensors, allowing them to act as a sort of selective mine. How a grenade is triggered is determined at the time of activation, and all grenades can easily be bought without any functional Matrix connection - preventing "unwanted accidents" by deckers.
