Baby Monitor

The Baby Monitor program uses algorithms stolen from the Grid Overwatch Division and keeps track of movements in the Matrix.

System: While the Baby Monitor is slotted, you are informed when someone is actively looking for you or your allies in the Matrix. Additionally, you upgrade all Computers checks when you use the Seek matrix action to search for adversaries that are actively hiding while having unauthorized access to a device or host that you own or are protecting.

Biofeedback

This program laces all of the attacks you make against other icons with especially dangerous biofeedback signals, the sort of drek that Black IC and G-men use.

System: When your Data Spike would cause biofeedback, you can spend [(aa)] to inflict a Critical Injury on the target.

Blackout

This is a kinder version of the Biofeedback cyberprogram, designed to incapacitate a target than outright kill them.

System: When your Data Spike would cause biofeedback, it deals strain instead of wounds.

Bootstrap

An IT expert’s tool of choice when working with boot code of a device. Corrupted by many hackers, Bootstrap allows for hidden commands to be input into the device after the next reboot. Popular options include announcing the location of the device when it connects to the Matrix, adjusting the processing of the device so that the default device attributes are the choice of the user, annoying the user with constant demands for ownership permissions, and making a record of every action on the device.

System: While you have access to a device, you can make an Edit File matrix action. On a success, until the end of the encounter or scene, you can set a single permission or configuration of the target device as if you were it's owner. Uncancelled [(s)] generated on the check increase the it's duration by an additional six hours, while [(aa)] can be used to set an additional permission or setting of the target device (this can be taken multiple times, requiring [(aa)] each).

Cat's Paw

A low-offensive attack program that distracts the user instead of damaging the device he is using, Cat’s Paw is useful to prevent a user from performing Matrix actions while not bricking the device they may be using. This program fills the AR display or VR experience with annoying errors, spam pop-ups and pop-unders, or other distracting garbage that may not be directy attributed to a hacking attempt.

System: As an action, you can make a Hacking combat check against a target persona. On a success, until the beginning of your next round, they are Disoriented.

Crash

One thing deckers have learned about the new Matrix protocols is that the ability to force devices to reboot can be very beneficial. Crash is there to help deckers who want to do this. When running, it attempts to fill a targeted device with an exceptional amount of reboot-worthy errors, causing it to crash and reboot.

System: As an action, you can make a Hard ([ddd]) Hacking skill check against a target that you have access to. On a success, the target crashes without breaking, and cannot be used for the rest of the encounter.

Data Bomb

System: As an action, make a Hacking skill check against a target icon that you have access to. On a success, it becomes protected by a Data Bomb. When someone else than you or unauthorized by you attempts to interact with the Data Bomb, they must succeed an Opposed Computers vs. Hacking skill check or suffer an amount of System Strain equal to your Intellect characteristic, plus any uncancelled [(s)] on the original check. If they succeed the Computers check, they do not trigger the bomb, and can take an Opposed Computers vs. Hacking skill check to disarm the bomb. When you set the bomb, you can decide if it should destroy the icon that it is protecting or not.

Decryption

System: While this program is active, you upgrade all Hacking skill checks when you use the Access System matrix action once.

Defuse

This program creates predictive barriers between you and a Data Bomb, making it easier and safer to disassemble.

System: While this program is active, you upgrade all Computers checks when interacting with a Data Bomb.

Exploit

System: When you add [[b]] to your next Hacking skill check due to the Mark matrix maneuver, you can add [(sa)] to the result of the check instead.

Fly on the Wall

Haven’t you always wanted to be the fly on a wall, in order to see the look on the faces of people when the final tally of their lives comes up? Well, GOD tends to prevent long-term surveillance by unauthorized personnel, but this program helps rebalance the scales.

System: As an action, you can designate an area roughly the size of a big room within Short range of your persona and place a small, hidden icon within it. While the Fly on the Wall is slotted and active, you are informed about all activity inside of the pool that can leave a digital trail, such as personas, devices and other icons of interest. Spotting the Fly takes a Seek matrix action, any you may make Seek action as if you were at the position of the Fly while the program is active.

Fork

System: While this program is active, you can increase the difficulty of a Computers or Hacking check by one in order to target an additional target within range. Use the higher of the target's difficulty and Firewall ratings to determine the total dice pool. On a success, the decker may spend [(aa)] to target an additional target.

Guard

This program keeps an eye out for weaknesses the same way an attacker would, making it harder for hostile deckers to place a mark on you.

System: While this program is active, as an Out-of-Turn incidental, you can suffer 2 strain and remove a mark that has been placed by the Mark matrix maneuver on you or an ally within Short range of your persona. Until the beginning of your next turn, no Mark matrix maneuvers can be taken against your protected target.

Hammer

System: While this program is active, you can increase the damage of your Data Spikes by two. If the target does not have a System Strain monitor, you can decrease the amount of [(s)] needed to damage the target by one step once.

Mugger

This program is often paired with Hammer programs in hit-and-run attacks against unsuspecting victims.

System: When your character uses the Mark matrix action to attack an unaware target with a Data Spike, they deal an additional two system strain to their target.

Paintjob

This program makes overlays your marks with AROs, marking not only their personas but also outlining them in realspace.

System: While this program is active, until the beginning of your next turn, after you have used the Mark matrix maneuver but have not used and Hacking combat or skill check against the marked target, you allies add [[b]] to their next Perception or combat check against the marked target.

Shell

System: While this program is active, you add [[ss]] to all Computers and Hacking skill checks you make, but reduce the amount of wounds and strain you would take through biofeedback by one. Additionally, if biofeedback or a jack-out would cause you to suffer a Critical Injury, you can subtract 30 from the roll to determine the injury.

Smoke-and-Mirrors

This program adds significant amounts of misleading location information in order to keep the decker from being located.

System: While this program is active, you upgrade all Hacking skill checks when you use the Hide matrix action once, but increase the difficulty of all other Computers and Hacking checks once. When you unslot this program, anyone actively looking for you with the Seek matrix action immediately becomes aware of you.

Sneak

System: While this program is active, a decker must spend an additional [(a)] in order to assess your physical location.
