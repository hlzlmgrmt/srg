Finding Concealed Items

Since most Shadowrunners try to keep their iron hidden from unwanted attention, it is often crucial for any runner to hide their equipment decently well. The most important factor for this is the size of the piece of equipment, represented by their Encumbrance rating. Usually, weapons with an Encumbrance of 1 or lower are easily hidden, requiring an active search or scanners to be found. Anything larger than that requires a little more work to effectively conceal, while items with an Encumbrance of 4 or more as generally impossible to hide.

A character or a scanner attempts to find an item that has been actively hidden by another character must succeed an Opposed Perception vs. Skulduggery check. The character attempting the search must specify what exactly it is that they are looking for, e.g. dangerous objects in general, or a piece of smuggled equipment in particular. Based on the most Encumbrance rating of the most cumbersome item (not to be confused with the Cumbersome quality), the character attempting the search adds [[b]] for each Encumbrance rating beyond one, and one [[s]] for each Concealable rating of the item.
